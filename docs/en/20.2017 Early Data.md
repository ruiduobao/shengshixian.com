## 2017 Administrative Division Change Areas

The 2021 and 2022 data were based on the 2020 administrative division base map, modified in chronological order. Data prior to 2020 requires backward deduction; that is, the 2017 early administrative division data references the administrative changes of the entire year of 2018, rather than the changes of 2016.

First are the regions where administrative boundaries were not changed, including the revocation of counties to establish districts or county-level cities. This part mainly involves the modification of attribute information (including place name attributes, division code attributes, county type attributes, county-level code attributes, former name attributes, and corresponding English attributes). Not all are displayed in maps; the main information of the changed attributes has been listed in the text.

Next are the areas with changes, which were updated by referencing the national basic information 2015 administrative divisions (with a currentness of 2012), the Ministry of Civil Affairs 2018 division data, and 2013 administrative division data (source unknown).

#### Province-level Type Visualization

As of January 1, 2017, China has 34 province-level units, consisting of 4 Municipalities, 23 Provinces, 5 Autonomous Regions, and 2 Special Administrative Regions (SARs).

![Early 2017 Province Types](/img/PICS/630b563a47aa6.png)

#### Prefecture-level Type Visualization

![Early 2017 Prefecture-level Unit Categories](/img/PICS/63a3e8ddd2d7c.png)

As of January 1, 2017, China has 334 prefecture-level units, consisting of 293 Prefecture-level Cities, 8 Prefectures, 30 Autonomous Prefectures, and 3 Leagues. Additionally, there are 36 vector elements not included in the statistics (e.g., Beijing Municipality, Macao SAR, Ding'an County directly governed by the province, etc.).

![Early 2017 Prefecture-level Unit Categories](/img/PICS/63a3ea18d4f05.png)

#### County-level Type Visualization

![Early 2017 County-level Administrative Division Types](/img/PICS/63a3eb5956253.png)

As of January 1, 2017, China has 2,862 vector elements, consisting of 1,365 Counties, 361 County-level Cities, 956 Districts, 117 Autonomous Counties, 49 Banners, 3 Autonomous Banners, 1 Special District, and 1 Forestry Area. Additionally, there are 9 vector elements not included in the county-level category statistics (e.g., Hong Kong SAR, prefecture-level cities with no county-level units like Zhongshan City and Dongguan City, etc.).
