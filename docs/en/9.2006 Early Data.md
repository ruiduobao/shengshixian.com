## CTAmap Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2006

## Update Overview

During 2006, a total of 6 counties and county-level cities were converted to districts, 4 districts were merged into 2 districts, 3 county-level units were renamed, and 4 county-level units had townships transferred out.

### District Mergers in 2006

### Heilongjiang Province

| Original Administrative Unit | Province/City | State Council Approval Date | Type of Change | New Administrative Entity | Approval Document |
| -------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ---------------- |
| Yonghong District; Jiaoqu | Jiamusi City, Heilongjiang Province | Jul 27 approved by the State Council of the People's Republic of China, Sep 7 announced by Jiamusi Municipal People's Government | Abolish Yonghong District of Jiamusi City, transfer its administrative area to the jurisdiction of Jiaoqu of Jiamusi City | Jiaoqu | State Council Letter [2006] No. 63 |
| Dongli District; Xiangfang District | Harbin City, Heilongjiang Province | Aug 15 approved by the State Council of the People's Republic of China, Oct 24 announced by Harbin Municipal People's Government | Abolish Dongli District and Xiangfang District of Harbin City, establish new Xiangfang District | Xiangfang District | State Council Letter [2006] No. 73 |

Partial administrative division adjustment of Jiamusi City, Heilongjiang Province: Abolish Yonghong District of Jiamusi City, transfer its administrative area to the jurisdiction of Jiaoqu of Jiamusi City. The Jiaoqu People's Government is located on Youyi Road. Yonghong District originally had 4 subdistricts: Chang'an Subdistrict, Jiaxi Subdistrict, Jianshe Subdistrict, and Youyi Subdistrict.



![](/img/PICS/06-14.png)



Abolish Dongli District and Xiangfang District of Harbin City, establish new Xiangfang District. The administrative areas of the original Dongli District and Xiangfang District form the administrative area of the newly established Xiangfang District. The Xiangfang District People's Government is located on Xiangfang Street. Dongli District has 12 subdistricts, 1 town, 43 residents' committees, and 25 administrative villages: Jianzhu Subdistrict, Hapinglu Subdistrict, Anle Subdistrict, Jiankanglu Subdistrict, Daqinglu Subdistrict, Jinxiang Subdistrict, Tongxiang Subdistrict, Hepinglu Subdistrict, Minshenglu Subdistrict, Wenzheng Subdistrict, Wangzhao Subdistrict, Liming Subdistrict, Chaoyang Town.
