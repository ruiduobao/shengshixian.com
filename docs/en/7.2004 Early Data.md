## CTAmap Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2004

## Update Overview

During 2004, a total of 1 prefecture (地区) was changed to a prefecture-level city (its subordinate 1 county was changed to a district), 2 new districts were established, 1 district was abolished, and 14 county-level units had their administrative division or names changed.
