## CTAmap Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2005

## Update Overview

During 2005, a total of 2 districts were abolished, 3 new districts were established, 5 districts and 1 county were renamed, and 2 districts were merged into 1 district.

## Specific Update Content
