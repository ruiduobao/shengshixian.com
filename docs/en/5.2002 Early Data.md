## CTAmap Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2002

## Update Overview

During 2002, a total of 10 prefectures were converted to prefecture-level cities (at the same time, 10 subordinate county-level units were changed to 11 county-level units, and 1 county-level unit was renamed), and the administrative divisions and names of 42 county-level units were changed.

![Regions with administrative changes in 2002](/img/PICS/680b8d3ff10c3.png)
