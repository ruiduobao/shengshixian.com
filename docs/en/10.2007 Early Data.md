## CTAmap Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2007

## Update Overview

During 2007, a total of 2 municipal districts, 1 autonomous county, and 1 prefecture-level city changed their names; a county-level city subordinate to 1 autonomous prefecture and a municipal district subordinate to 1 prefecture-level city were merged to form a municipal district of a prefecture-level city.

### Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region

| Original Administrative Unit | Subordinate Province/City                       | Date of State Council Approval                  | Type of Change                                           | New Administrative Setup       | Approval Document             |
| ---------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- |
| Miquan City     | Changji Hui Autonomous Prefecture, Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region | Approved by the State Council of the People's Republic of China on June 30 | Abolish Miquan City and Dongshan District of Urumqi City, and establish Midong District of Urumqi City | Midong District of Urumqi City | State Council Letter [2007] No. 65 |
| Dongshan District     | Urumqi City, Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region     | Same as above                            | Same as above                                               | Same as above             | Same as above             |



Abolish Miquan City and Dongshan District of Urumqi City, and establish Midong District of Urumqi City. The administrative area of Midong District is the former administrative area of Miquan City and Dongshan District of Urumqi City.

(1) Miquan City: Before the merger it had jurisdiction over 3 subdistricts, 5 towns and 1 ethnic township: Donglu Subdistrict, Xilu Subdistrict, Youhaolu Subdistrict, Gumudi Town, Tiechanggou Town, Changshanzi Town, Yangmaogong Town, Sandaoba Town, and Baiyanghe Kazakh Township.

![](/img/PICS/07-05.png)



(2) Dongshan District: Before the merger it had jurisdiction over 3 subdistricts and 1 township: Shihua Subdistrict, Dibang Subdistrict, Kaziwang Subdistrict, and Lucaogou Township.

![](/img/PICS/07-06.png)
