# Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2018

## Preface

This dataset reflects the administrative divisions of China in early 2018. If you need data from early 2019 to early 2022, please visit: **shengshixian.com** (a pun on "province / city / county" in Chinese).

## Administrative Division Changes in Early 2018

The 2021 and 2022 datasets are based on the 2020 administrative division basemap and are modified chronologically. Data before 2020, however, must be reconstructed retroactively — that is, the early 2018 administrative division data is referenced against the changes made throughout the year 2018, not the 2017 changes.

First, areas where administrative boundaries were not changed — including counties being abolished to become districts or county-level cities — primarily involve updates to attribute information (including place name, division code, county type, county code, former name attributes, and the corresponding English attributes). These are not all illustrated with maps; the main attribute updates are listed in the text.

Then, areas with boundary changes were updated by referencing the National Fundamental Geographic Information 2015 administrative divisions (currency 2012), the National Fundamental Geographic Information 2021 administrative divisions (currency 2019), the Ministry of Civil Affairs 2018 division data, and the 2013 administrative division data (no source).
