# Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2012

## CTAmap Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2012 (as of 2011-12-31)

## Update Overview

During 2012, 6 counties and county-level cities were converted to districts, 9 districts were merged into 4 districts, 5 new county-level units and 1 new prefecture-level city were established, and some townships of 3 county-level units were transferred to 3 county-level units. Based on the specific update content and the administrative division data from early 2013, a reverse derivation was performed to obtain the province/city/county administrative division data for early 2012.

## Specific Update Content

### Suzhou City, Jiangsu Province

Abolish Canglang District, Pingjiang District, and Jinchang District of Suzhou City, establish Gusu District of Suzhou City. The administrative areas of the original Canglang District, Pingjiang District, and Jinchang District form the administrative area of Gusu District.

|Original Administrative Unit|Province/City|Date of Change|Type of Change|New Administrative Entity|Approval Document|

In early 2012, Canglang District had 6 subdistricts: Shuangta Subdistrict, Nanmen Subdistrict, Xujiang Subdistrict, Wumenqiao Subdistrict, Fengmen Subdistrict, and Youxin Subdistrict. Among them, in 2012, the original Nanmen Subdistrict was renamed Canglang Subdistrict. In 2017, the original Canglang Subdistrict and Xujiang Subdistrict merged to form a new Canglang Subdistrict.

In early 2012, Pingjiang District had 6 subdistricts: Guanqian Subdistrict, Pingjianglu Subdistrict, Sujin Subdistrict, Loumen Subdistrict, Chengbei Subdistrict, and Taohuawu Subdistrict.

In early 2012, Jinchang District had 5 subdistricts: Shilu Subdistrict, Liuyuan Subdistrict, Caixiang Subdistrict, Baiyangwan Subdistrict, and Huqiu Subdistrict. Among them, Caixiang Subdistrict was renamed Jinchang Subdistrict on Dec 21, 2012.

### Guiyang City, Guizhou Province

Abolish Huaxi District and Xiaohe District of Guiyang City, establish new Huaxi District of Guiyang City. The administrative areas of the original Huaxi District and Xiaohe District form the administrative area of the new Huaxi District.

|Original Administrative Unit|Province/City|Date of Change|Type of Change|New Administrative Entity|Approval Document|
| --------------| --------------| --------------------------------| ------------------------------------------------| ------------| -------------------|
|Huaxi District, Xiaohe District|Guiyang City, Guizhou Province|Nov 15 approved by the State Council, Nov 24 announced|Abolish Huaxi District and Xiaohe District of Guiyang City, establish new Huaxi District of Guiyang City|Huaxi District|State Council Letter [2012] No. 190|

Xiaohe District underwent very complex administrative division changes in the years before 2012, and its subordinate areas were rather fragmented. The division here only includes the areas registered with the Ministry of Civil Affairs, excluding entrusted areas. For example, Xiaohe District once entrusted Chenliang Village, Wengyan Village, and Fuguan Village (3 administrative villages) of Huaxi District, which are not counted as formal areas here.

Xiaohe District's jurisdiction includes these five areas registered with the Ministry of Civil Affairs: **Sanjiang Subdistrict, Pingqiao Subdistrict, Jinzhu Subdistrict, Changjiang Subdistrict, and Huanghe Subdistrict**. Among them, in 2010, 8 residents' committees under Changjiang Subdistrict Office and 6 residents' committees under Huanghe Subdistrict Office were consolidated to form 4 new communities: Qianjiang Community, Qingpu Community, Ruihua Community, and Xinglong Community.

![Areas under the jurisdiction of Xiaohe District in early 2012](/img/PICS/network-asset-66b5a0ee36519-20240813165951-txz062c.png)​

Xiaohe District has a total of 12 subordinate communities (Qianjiang Community, Qingpu Community, Ruihua Community, Xinglong Community, Sanjiang Community, Pingqiao Community, Hangtian Community, Xiaomeng Community, Hangkong Community, Jinzhu Community, Jinxin Community, and Huanghe Community).

Based on this, the administrative division boundaries of Huaxi District and Xiaohe District in early 2012 were obtained.

### Establishment of Prefecture-level Sansha City in Hainan Province

Abolish the Xisha Islands, Nansha Islands, and Zhongsha Islands Offices of Hainan Province, and establish prefecture-level Sansha City, with jurisdiction over the islands, reefs, and sea areas of the Xisha Islands, Zhongsha Islands, and Nansha Islands.

|Province/City|Date of Change|Type of Change|New Administrative Entity|Approval Document|
| ----------| -------------------| --------------------------------------------------------------| ------------| ------------|
|Hainan Province|Jun 21 approved by the State Council|Abolish the Xisha Islands, Nansha Islands, and Zhongsha Islands Offices of Hainan Province, establish prefecture-level Sansha City|Sansha City|MCA Announcement|

Among them, in 1988, Hainan became a province, and its jurisdiction included 19 cities and counties on Hainan Island and the islands, reefs, and sea areas of the Xisha Islands, Nansha Islands, and Zhongsha Islands. On Sep 19, it was renamed to "Hainan Province Xisha Islands, Nansha Islands, and Zhongsha Islands Office", which was equivalent to a county-level institution but did not constitute a formal county-level administrative entity. It was not until 2012 that Sansha City was formally established as a prefecture-level administrative division.
