## CTAmap Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2003

## Update Overview

During 2003, a total of 3 prefectures and 3 leagues were converted into prefecture-level cities (at the same time, 4 counties and 1 county-level city under their jurisdiction were converted into districts), 1 county was converted into a prefecture-level city (1 county and 1 county-level city were transferred from two prefecture-level cities, and districts were established in the original county-level areas), 5 new districts were established, and 24 county-level units underwent administrative division and name changes.
