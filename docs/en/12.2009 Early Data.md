# Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2009 (as of 2008.12.31)

## CTAmap Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2009

## Update Overview

During 2009, a total of 1 county-level city was changed into a municipal district, 1 municipal district was abolished, 3 municipal districts were merged into 1 municipal district, 1 county and 1 municipal district were newly established, and 3 villages subordinate to 3 towns of 1 county were transferred to 1 autonomous county.

## Specific Update Content

### Sichuan Province, Mianyang City

| Transfer-out Party | Subordinate Superior | Date of MCA Approval | Type of Change | Transfer-in Party | Approval Document |
| --------| --------------| ----------------| -------------------------------------------------------------| ----------------| ------------------|
| Anxian County | Mianyang City, Sichuan Province | February 1 | Anchang Town, Yong'an Town, and 6 villages of Huangtu Town of Anxian County shall be transferred to the jurisdiction of Beichuan Qiang Autonomous County | Beichuan Qiang Autonomous County | MCA Letter [2009] No. 41 |

Beichuan Qiang Autonomous County's administrative division was adjusted. Anchang Town, Yong'an Town of Anxian County, and Changle, Hongyan, Shunyi, Hongqi, Wenquan, Dongyu 6 villages of Huangtu Town were transferred to the jurisdiction of Beichuan Qiang Autonomous County.

![Administrative Division Map of Anxian County, Early 2009](/img/PICS/network-asset-66bb671c1e75b-20240814143659-aje1m92.webp)​

Among them, Beichuan Qiang Autonomous County abolished the designations of the 6 villages of Changle, Hongyan, Shunyi, Hongqi, Wenquan, Dongyu. The population of the whole town was uniformly planned as urban population, and Yongchang Town was established. From this, the vector data of Anxian County's administrative divisions in early 2009 was obtained.

Industrial Zones: Kangqiao Industrial Zone, Shanghai Nanhui Industrial Zone, Nanhui Binhai Tourism Resort, Shanghai International Medical Park, Nanhui Modern Agriculture Park, Shanghai Marine High-tech Development Zone.

Liuzao Town has jurisdiction over Lucheng Residents' Committee, Luxin Residents' Committee, Huilong Village Committee, Xinji Village Committee, Qicheng Village Committee, Qizao Village Committee, Minyi Village Committee, Tangdian Village Committee, Luxi Village Committee, Lianmin Village Committee, Chenqiao Village Committee, Guoyuan Village Committee.
