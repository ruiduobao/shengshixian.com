This is a major version update. The update contents are as follows:

(1) The administrative divisions of each year have been checked one by one against the records of the Ministry of Civil Affairs.

(2) The `former-name` and `remarks` attributes have been updated extensively.

(3) The boundaries of some regions have been changed, and the topology errors of some regions have been corrected.

The workload of this update is large, and the changes are substantial. Readers using the province/city/county data are advised to download the latest version of the data.

## Former-Name Attribute Update

### Former-Name and Remarks Attribute Update for Prefecture-Level Data

The `former-name` and `remarks` attributes of the prefecture-level data existed in the very first version. Considering that there are relatively few renamings of prefecture-level administrative divisions, these attributes were once removed. When producing the 2015–2018 data, several prefecture-level administrative-division changes were encountered, so the attributes were added back.

## Province/City/County Count Correction

For each year of this dataset, the administrative divisions are based on the changes up to December 31 of the previous year. For example, the early 2023 division data has an actual statistical cut-off date of December 31, 2022, and can also be referred to as the end-of-2022 division data.

On the basis of making this clear, the counts of provinces, cities, and counties are referenced against the Ministry of Civil Affairs' Administrative-Division Statistics Table (http://xzqh.mca.gov.cn/statistics/2018.html), which is produced on December 31 of each year. With reference to this table, the counts in the early-2015 to early-2019 division data have been checked, deleted, and modified.

### (2) Yeji District, Anhui Province, 2015

On October 13, 2015, the "State Council Approval on Adjusting the Administrative Divisions of Parts of Tongling City, Lu'an City, and Anqing City, Anhui Province" (State Council Letter [2015] No. 181) was issued, agreeing to adjust the administrative divisions of parts of Anqing City, Tongling City, Lu'an City, and Huainan City, and to establish Yeji District of Lu'an City. Yejizhen, Sanyuan Town, and Sungang Township of Huoqiu County are transferred to the jurisdiction of Yeji District, and Yeji is converted from a functional area to an administrative area.
In August 2016, the Anhui Provincial Civil Affairs Department's "Letter on Agreeing to the Adjustment of Parts of the Administrative Divisions between Yeji District and Huoqiu County, Lu'an City" and the Lu'an Municipal Government's "Implementation Opinions on the Transfer of Yaoli Town and Hongji Town of Huoqiu County to the Jurisdiction of Yeji District" agreed to transfer Yaoli Town and Hongji Town of Huoqiu County to the jurisdiction of Yeji District. 

![Adjustment of Yeji District](/img/PICS/63c3cbb57e0a6.jpg)

In the previous 2016–2023 administrative-division data, Yaoli Town and Hongji Town were missed in Yeji District. The data has been modified in this version.

## Topology Error Correction

In updating the data for each year, operations such as splitting, clipping, and merging were used, and a few areas have dangles, voids, etc.

Previously, a netizen reported that the data had topology errors. I could not find them using ArcGIS and ignored them, which actually caused significant data quality issues.

### 2023 Administrative-Division Change

After a gap of 15 months, China finally ushered in the first adjustment of administrative divisions at or above the county level. The adjusted area is the Ninth Division of the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps.

The Ninth Division of the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps has jurisdiction over the 161st Regiment, 162nd Regiment, 164th Regiment, 165th Regiment, 166th Regiment, 167th Regiment, 168th Regiment (including the 169th Regiment), 170th Regiment, and Tuanjie Farm.

Baiyang City was established on January 20, 2023, evolved from the former Ninth Division of the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps. The name comes from the Xiaobaiyang Sentinel Post under its jurisdiction. It is a county-level city under the jurisdiction of the Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region. The division's headquarters is located in Emin County, actually within the scope of Tacheng City, Xinjiang, adjacent to the urban area of Tacheng City and the Kazakhstan Bakhty Port of China.

The county-level Baiyang City newly established in 2023 has 3 administrative areas in total, all distributed along the national border, as follows:

1. Parts of the administrative area of Tacheng City (the defined areas of the 163rd Regiment and 164th Regiment) are transferred to the jurisdiction of the proposed county-level Baiyang City.

2. Parts of the administrative area of Emin County (the defined area of the 165th Regiment) are transferred to the jurisdiction of the proposed county-level Baiyang City.

3. Parts of the administrative area of Yumin County (the defined area of the 161st Regiment) are transferred to the jurisdiction of the proposed county-level Baiyang City.

The administrative area of the proposed county-level Baiyang City covers 2034.45 square kilometers, of which 289.08 square kilometers are transferred from Tacheng City involving the 163rd Regiment and 164th Regiment, 599.64 square kilometers are transferred from Emin County involving the 165th Regiment, and 1145.73 square kilometers are transferred from Yumin County involving the 161st Regiment. The Ninth Division has signed land title confirmation agreements with Tacheng City, Emin County, and Yumin County for the above areas.

## Replies to Questions

(1) A netizen reported that some regions have no administrative-division data.

![](/img/PICS/63c3880e45073.png)

Answer: Huzhong District and Xinlin District are under the Forestry Bureau of the Daxinganling Forestry Group Company of the State Forestry Administration. They are part of a government-enterprise integrated system and have not formally entered the administrative divisions of the Ministry of Civil Affairs. This data is updated based on the divisions of the Ministry of Civil Affairs. Most forest farms, agricultural farms, development zones, high-tech zones, management committees, industrial parks, industrial zones, management areas, etc. are not in the administrative divisions of the Ministry of Civil Affairs. They are non-formal administrative divisions and are not included in the scope of consideration. They will not be updated in the future either. If some regions are assigned to the formal administrative divisions of the Ministry of Civil Affairs in the future, I will update them in the data.

(2) Netizens hope that some village-level data can be provided.

![](/img/PICS/63c388c26465c.png)

Answer: I have village-level data for a few provinces, but considering that data at this level involves classified information, I will not share it.
