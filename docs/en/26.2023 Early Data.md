## Background of Administrative-Division Changes

Since 2022, changes in administrative divisions at or above the county level have **slowed down significantly**, with **no new abolishment of counties or establishment of districts/cities**.

In March 2022, the "Government Work Report" explicitly stated "strictly control the abolishment of counties to establish cities or districts."

In May 2022, the "Opinions on Promoting Urbanization with County Towns as Important Carriers" explicitly stated "**strictly control the abolishment of counties to establish cities or districts**."

In June 2022, the Central Comprehensive Deepening Reform Commission emphasized "treating the renaming of administrative divisions prudently, and not changing old place names at will."

Therefore, during 2022, there were **only 4** jurisdictional adjustments between county-level administrative divisions, with no renaming or county-level type modifications.

![Number of county-level division changes from 2000 to 2022](/img/PICS/63aecac401c52.png)

Since then, the **place-renaming frenzy that lasted for more than a decade and attracted many regions** has come to a temporary pause.

## Areas of Administrative-Division Change in 2023

The statistical cut-off date for the early-2023 administrative-division data is January 1, 2023. This data is based on the 2020 administrative-division basemap (https://www.shengshixian.com/pages/b8a988) and the early-2023 administrative-division data was produced in chronological order for 2000 to 2023.

For areas with modified boundaries, the boundary vectors have been updated with reference to the 2021 National Fundamental Information administrative divisions (currency 2019), the 2013 administrative-division data, the national 2013 township boundary data and the 2022 Amap (AutoNavi) township administrative-division data, the 2022 village-level vector data of Henan Province (from Tianditu), and the 2022 village-level vector data of Jiangxi Province. The jurisdictional adjustments between administrative divisions listed are all jurisdictional adjustments between administrative divisions registered with the Ministry of Civil Affairs, and do not include trusteeship or custodial situations.

### Other

In 2022, jurisdictional adjustments of trusteeship or custody equivalent to county-level-and-above management areas include: Mengla County's Mohan Town of Xishuangbanna Dai Autonomous Prefecture under Kunming City, Yunnan Province; the Tibet Airport New Area being changed from entrusted to Lhasa City to managed by Shannon City; Shangchuan Town of Yongdeng County, Lanzhou City, Gansu Province being subject to the overall trusteeship of Lanzhou New Area; Weichuan Town and Daying Town of Weishi County, Kaifeng City, Henan Province being subject to the overall trusteeship of Zhengzhou Airport Area on behalf; Baimasi Town of Luolong District, Luoyang City, Henan Province being entrusted to Chanhe Hui District for custody; and subdistricts and towns under the custody of Luoyang High-tech Zone, Henan Province being returned to Luolong District and Jianxi District, etc.

However, **there is an essential difference between administrative trusteeship and transfer-in**. Administrative trusteeship is temporary and can be changed at any time. Transfer-in is generally permanent and cannot be changed at will. **Therefore, the trusteeship areas are not reflected in the data.**
