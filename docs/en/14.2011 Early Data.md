# Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2011

## CTAmap Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2011 (as of 2010.12.31)

## Update Overview

During 2011, China had a total of 3 counties changed into municipal districts, 3 county-level cities changed into municipal districts, 1 municipal district abolished, 6 districts/counties merged into 3 municipal districts, 1 county-level city newly established, 1 prefecture-level city abolished (and simultaneously 1 municipal district subordinate to it changed into a county-level city, with the new county-level city and 4 other subordinate counties assigned to 3 prefecture-level cities), 2 prefectures changed into prefecture-level cities, and 1 municipal district renamed.

Based on the administrative division changes that occurred during 2011 and the province-city-county administrative division vector data of early 2012, the production of the province-city-county administrative division vector data of early 2011 has begun.
