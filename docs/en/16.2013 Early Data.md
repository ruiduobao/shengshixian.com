# Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2013

## CTAmap Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2013 (as of 2012.12.31)

## Update Content

During 2013, 8 counties and 3 county-level cities in China were changed into districts, 3 counties were changed into county-level cities, 8 districts were merged into 4 districts, 5 districts were newly established, 1 prefecture was changed into a prefecture-level city, and 1 district was renamed. On the basis of the end-of-2013 province/city/county data, we performed a reverse derivation to obtain the province/city/county administrative division data for the beginning of 2013. The specific modification process is described below.
