## CTAmap Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2000

## Update Overview

During 2000, a total of 21 prefectures were converted into prefecture-level cities (simultaneously, 21 counties and county-level cities under them were converted into 22 districts, and 2 county-level units were merged into 1 county-level city), 1 prefecture-level city was renamed, and 2 province-directly-governed county-level cities were converted into prefecture-level cities (simultaneously, districts were established in the original county-level city areas, and 3 counties and 1 county-level city were placed under their jurisdiction). In addition, 11 counties and county-level cities were converted into 13 districts, 1 district was abolished, 3 new districts were established, 7 districts were renamed, and 2 development zones in municipalities were abolished.
