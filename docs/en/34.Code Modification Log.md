### Suizhou City (随州市)

At the end of 1999, Suizhou City was a county-level city directly governed by Hubei Province. In June 2000, it was upgraded from a province-directly-governed county-level city to a prefecture-level city, with the simultaneous establishment of Zengdu District (曾都区) and the incorporation of Guangshui City (广水市).
