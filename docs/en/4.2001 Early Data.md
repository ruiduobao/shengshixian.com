## CTAmap Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2001

## Update Overview

During 2001, a total of 4 prefectures and 2 leagues (盟) were changed to prefecture-level cities (while the 6 subordinate county-level cities were changed to districts), 1 prefecture was abolished, 16 counties and county-level cities were changed to districts, 7 districts were abolished, 7 new districts were established, and 4 county-level units were renamed.

![Areas of administrative changes in 2001](/img/PICS/680b8cdee22d7.png)
