# Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2015
## Preface

This data is the administrative division data for the beginning of 2015. If you need data from the beginning of 2016 to the beginning of 2022, please visit: **shengshixian.com** (a homophone of province-city-county).

![Province-City-County Data Download](/img/PICS/63a6ec4ee0f14.png)

During 2015, **22 counties and 4 county-level cities were changed into districts, 5 counties were changed into county-level cities, 11 districts were merged into 5 districts, 1 county-level city and 2 districts were newly established, 2 counties had their superior prefecture-level cities changed, 1 county-level city was changed into a prefecture-level city, 2 prefectures were changed into prefecture-level cities** (the 2 subordinate county-level units were changed into districts), **1 district was renamed**, and 1 prefecture-level city and 1 county changed their government seats.

## 2015 Administrative Division Changed Areas

The 2021 and 2022 data are based on the 2020 administrative division basemap produced, and are modified in chronological order. Data prior to 2020 needs to be back-derived, that is, the administrative division data at the beginning of 2015 is referenced to the administrative division changes throughout 2015, not the 2015 administrative division changes.

First, areas whose administrative boundaries have not been changed, including county abolitions and establishments of districts and county-level cities; this part mainly involves modifications of attribute information (including place name attribute, administrative division code attribute, county type attribute, county code attribute, former name attribute, and corresponding English attributes). Not all are shown in maps; the main changed attribute information has been listed in the text.

Then, areas with changed boundaries, with reference to the National Fundamental Information 2015 administrative divisions (currency 2012), the Ministry of Civil Affairs 2018 administrative division data, 2013 administrative division data (no source), and the 2013 national township-level boundary data for data updates.
