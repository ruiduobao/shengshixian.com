# Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2010

## CTAmap Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2010 (as of 2009.12.31)

## Update Overview

During 2010, a total of 4 municipal districts were merged into 2 municipal districts, 1 county was changed into a municipal district, 2 counties were changed into county-level cities, 1 municipal district was abolished, and 1 county-level city was established.

Based on the change situations, using the existing early 2011 administrative division data, a reverse deduction was performed to obtain the province-city-county administrative division data of early 2010.

## Specific Update Content

### Jiangsu Province, Xuzhou City

|Original Administrative Unit|Subordinate Superior Unit|Date of Approval|Type of Change|New Administrative Setup|Approval Document|
| ------------| --------------| ------------------| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------| ------------| ------------------|
|Jiuli District|Xuzhou City|Approved by the State Council on September 5|Abolish Jiuli District of Xuzhou City. Jiuli Subdistrict and Shidong Village of Pangzhuang Subdistrict shall be transferred to Gulou District, and the remaining parts shall be transferred to Quanshan District and Tongshan County|None|State Council Letter [2010] No. 90|
|Tongshan County|Xuzhou City|Approved by the State Council on September 5|Abolish Tongshan County, and establish Tongshan District of Xuzhou City|Tongshan District||

Abolish Jiuli District of Xuzhou City. Jiuli Subdistrict of the former Jiuli District and Shidong Village of Pangzhuang Subdistrict shall be transferred to the jurisdiction of Gulou District, Xuzhou City. Huohua, Sushan, Taoyuan, and Pangzhuang (excluding Shidong Village, Shixi Village, and Xinxin Residents' Committee) 4 subdistricts of the former Jiuli District shall be transferred to the jurisdiction of Quanshan District, Xuzhou City.

Abolish Tongshan County, and establish Tongshan District of Xuzhou City. Yi'an, Liguo, Zhangji, Chacheng, Dianchang, Zhangshuanglou, Sanhejian, Shitan 8 subdistricts of the former Jiuli District, as well as Shixi Village and Xinxin Residents' Committee of Pangzhuang Subdistrict, shall be transferred to the jurisdiction of Tongshan District.

After inquiry, the jurisdiction of the former Jiuli District in early 2010 was: Pangzhuang Subdistrict, Huohua Subdistrict, Liguo Subdistrict (Liqun Community), Taoyuan Subdistrict, Sanhejian Subdistrict, Chacheng Subdistrict, Yi'an Subdistrict, Zhangji Subdistrict, Dianchang Subdistrict, Zhangshuanglou Subdistrict (Shuanglou Community), Jiuli Subdistrict, Shitan Subdistrict, Sushan Subdistrict.

Please note:

Since some areas were former mining areas of Xuzhou, the jurisdiction is scattered with many exclaves.

Among them, Chacheng Subdistrict, including Chacheng Community Residents' Committee and Xincheng Community Residents' Committee, is an exclave within Liuxin Town.

![](/img/PICS/network-asset-66bb0292ec082-20240813165919-h6bmb24.webp)​

Dianchang Subdistrict, Yi'an Subdistrict, and Zhangji Subdistrict are all exclaves of Jiuli District (formerly known as Xuzhou Mining Area, renamed in 1993).

Dianchang Subdistrict is within Maocun Town of Tongshan District, and is too small. I searched the township-level and three sets of village-level vector data on hand but could not find it, and cannot verify it.

Yi'an Subdistrict is within Dapeng Town, and after searching the village and township data, it could not be found, and cannot be verified. It will be updated later when vector data of this area is available.

Zhangji Subdistrict is within Liuji Town, and also cannot be found, and cannot be verified for this area.

It will be updated later when the vector data of this area is collected.
