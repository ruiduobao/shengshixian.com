# Province/City/County Administrative Division Data, Early 2014

## Update Content

(1) Released national county-level and prefecture-level vector data as of the end of 2013;

(2) Corrected boundary errors for some regions in the 2014–2022 data.

The areas affected by this update are shown in the figure below:

![Areas affected by this update](/img/PICS/63e896d4a86a2.png)

## Preface

This dataset is the 2013 administrative-division data. If you need data from 2014 to 2023, please visit: **shengshixian.com** (homophone: 省市县, i.e. Province-City-County).

![shengshixian.com data download page](/img/PICS/63e89793a98b7.png)

During 2014, China had 24 counties and county-level cities converted into districts, 1 county converted into a county-level city, 8 counties/districts merged into 4 districts, 1 county and 1 district renamed, 6 towns merged into 4 districts, 2 county-level cities newly established, 3 township-level units from 3 county-level units transferred to 3 other county-level units, parts of 4 township-level units from 3 counties transferred to 1 county-level city, 1 district abolished, and 1 prefecture converted into a prefecture-level city.

The 2013 administrative-division data has a reference date of December 31, 2013. By retroactively applying the 2014 changes, the 2013 administrative-division data can be obtained.

## 2013 Administrative-Division Changed Areas

The 2021 and 2022 data are based on the 2020 administrative-division basemap produced earlier, modified in chronological order. Data for years before 2020 must be back-extrapolated, i.e. the 2013 administrative-division data is derived from the 2014 changes for the entire year, not the 2013 changes.

For areas whose administrative boundaries did not change — including counties abolished and set up as districts or county-level cities — only attribute information was updated (including place-name attribute, code attribute, county type attribute, county-code attribute, former-name attribute, and corresponding English attributes). Not all are shown graphically; the main updated attributes are listed in the text.
